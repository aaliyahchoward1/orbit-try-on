# Demo video script — Orbit Try-On (target: 2:15, hard max 3:00)

Rules from Devpost: 1–3 minutes, public on YouTube, name the YouCam API, show the app working end to end, **no copyrighted music** (use silence or YouTube Audio Library tracks).

Record on your phone with screen recording, or on a laptop with OBS or the built-in recorder. Use live mode, not demo mode. Use natural window light for your selfie.

---

**0:00–0:15 · Hook (show your necklace in hand, then the shop page)**
> "I make lip gloss necklaces by hand. Real gloss, inside a baroque vial. The number one question shoppers have is one a photo can't answer: what will this look like on me?"

**0:15–0:30 · What it is (open the app)**
> "Orbit Try-On answers that with the YouCam API. One selfie, my four real shades, on your own lips, then straight to the listing."

**0:30–0:55 · Upload + analysis**
Upload your selfie, tap analyze, show the loading state, then the color map.
> "YouCam's AI Skin Tone Analysis reads five colors from my face: skin, lips, eyes, brows and hair. The app turns my skin color into a depth and undertone estimate, and it's upfront that it's an estimate."

**0:55–1:35 · Try-on (the money shot)**
Pick a mood, show the ranked shades, try the top one, flip before/after, then try two more shades.
> "Then YouCam's Makeup Virtual Try-On puts the gloss on my lips. Here's Blood Moon... before, after. Nebula's Gaze. Black Siren goes on sheer, just like it does in real life."

**1:35–1:50 · Liner toggle**
Turn on "add liner".
> "Liner is applied in the same YouCam task, so it costs no extra calls."

**1:50–2:05 · Buy**
Tap "wear this shade" and show the Etsy listing load.
> "And when you find your shade, one tap takes you to the necklace."

**2:05–2:15 · Close**
> "Every color here was sampled from my real product photos, selfies are never stored, and it's built so a one-woman shop can afford to run it. Orbit Try-On, built on the YouCam API."
